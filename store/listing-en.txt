Drops farms Twitch drops for the games you choose. It does not play the stream. It sends the same minute-watched pings as the Twitch player, reads progress from Twitch, and claims a drop when Twitch marks it ready.

- Sign-in uses the Twitch session already open in this browser. The auth-token cookie is read locally. Your password is never stored.
- You pick the games. Only those campaigns are farmed.
- Exclusive drops are watched only on their listed streamers. Regular drops can run on any live channel with drops enabled.
- If the current streamer goes offline, the miner switches to someone who is live.
- The popup is available in English and Russian.

The farming approach follows TwitchDropsMiner by DevilXD. Sign-in was rebuilt around the browser cookie because device-code login was broken. Support for this extension is not guaranteed.
